Spa policies

Booking policy & service guarantee

Your comfort, confidence and satisfaction are important to us. To keep expectations clear and fair for everyone, we kindly ask all clients to review these policies.

A friendly reminder

  • Please allow at least 24 hours' notice for cancellations or rescheduling.
  • Late cancellations (less than 24 hours) are subject to a fee of 50% of the scheduled service price.
  • No-shows without prior notice are subject to a fee of 100%.
  • Genuine emergencies will always be considered individually.

Thank you for respecting our time and supporting our team!

Appointment, Cancellation & No‑Show Policy

At Vesna Beauty Spa, we value your time and appreciate your consideration of our team members' schedules.

When an appointment is booked, that time is reserved exclusively for you. Late cancellations and missed appointments affect our ability to accommodate other clients and result in lost time and income for our service providers.

To ensure fairness and respect for everyone's time, we kindly ask you to observe the following booking policies.

1. Appointment Cancellation & Rescheduling

We kindly require at least 24 hours' notice for any appointment cancellation or rescheduling.

If you need to change or cancel your appointment, please contact Vesna Beauty Spa as early as possible.

2. Late Cancellation

Cancellations or rescheduling requests made less than 24 hours before the scheduled appointment are subject to a late cancellation fee of 50% of the scheduled service price.

This fee helps compensate for the reserved appointment time that may no longer be possible to fill.

3. No-Show Policy

If a client fails to attend a scheduled appointment without providing prior notice, a no-show fee of 100% of the scheduled service price applies.

We reserve the right to request payment of the applicable fee before accepting future bookings.

4. Emergency Situations

We understand that unexpected circumstances and emergencies can happen.

Genuine emergencies and exceptional situations will always be considered individually and with understanding.

Our intention is to maintain fairness while treating every client with respect and compassion.

5. Our Commitment

We appreciate your understanding and cooperation.

By respecting appointment times and cancellation policies, you help us provide the best possible service and ensure that our team members' time is valued.

Thank you for choosing Vesna Beauty Spa and for being part of our community.

Vesna Beauty Spa Management

Client Policies & Service Guarantee

At Vesna Beauty Spa, we are committed to providing professional services, quality care and a welcoming experience for every guest.

Our team takes pride in its professional experience, attention to detail and dedication to client satisfaction. We believe that a positive spa experience is built on trust, communication and mutual respect.

1. Our Commitment to Quality

We strive to provide every service with professionalism, care and attention to detail.

Our services are performed by trained professionals following appropriate industry practices and procedures.

We are committed to listening to our clients, addressing concerns and maintaining high standards of service.

2. Service Satisfaction & Client Concerns

Your feedback matters to us.

If you are dissatisfied with a service or experience an unexpected result, please contact Vesna Beauty Spa as soon as reasonably possible.

To help us properly assess your concern, we may ask you to:

  • Provide details about the service and the concern.
  • Share photographs, where appropriate.
  • Return to the spa so we can personally assess the result.
  • Allow us to consult with the service provider who performed the treatment.

Every concern will be reviewed individually and given appropriate attention.

3. Individual Results & Expectations

Beauty and wellness treatments may produce different results depending on individual factors, including skin and nail conditions, lifestyle, natural body responses, maintenance and aftercare.

Although we follow professional procedures and provide appropriate recommendations, individual results and the longevity of certain services may vary.

A result that differs from personal expectations does not automatically indicate improper service or professional negligence.

4. Corrections & Service Adjustments

Where a concern related to the service provided is identified, Vesna Beauty Spa may offer an appropriate correction or adjustment, depending on the circumstances.

Any proposed resolution will be determined following a reasonable assessment of the situation.

We appreciate the opportunity to review and address concerns directly.

5. Refunds, Salon Credits & Compensation

Every complaint or request for compensation is reviewed individually and in good faith.

Dissatisfaction with a personal preference or an individual treatment result does not automatically entitle a client to a refund or complimentary service.

Depending on the circumstances, possible resolutions may include:

  • A service correction or adjustment.
  • A salon credit toward another service.
  • Another mutually agreed solution.
  • A refund where appropriate or required by applicable law.

Any compensation or special accommodation is assessed by Vesna Beauty Spa management.

Nothing in this policy is intended to limit or exclude any rights or remedies available to clients under applicable consumer protection laws.

6. Aftercare & Maintenance

Clients are encouraged to follow all aftercare instructions and professional recommendations provided by their service provider.

The longevity and results of certain treatments may be affected by individual conditions, lifestyle, external factors or failure to follow recommended aftercare.

Please contact us if you have questions about maintaining your results.

7. Respectful Communication

We are committed to maintaining a welcoming, respectful and professional environment for both our clients and team members.

We kindly ask that all communication with our staff remains courteous and respectful.

We are always willing to listen, discuss concerns and work toward a fair and reasonable resolution.

8. Our Promise

At Vesna Beauty Spa, we genuinely appreciate every client who chooses us.

Our goal is not simply to provide a service, but to build lasting relationships based on trust, professionalism, quality care and mutual respect.

Thank you for your understanding, your trust and for being part of the Vesna Beauty Spa community.

With appreciation,
The Vesna Beauty Spa Team
