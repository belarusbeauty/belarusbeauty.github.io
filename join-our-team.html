About Vesna Beauty Spa. Vesna Beauty Spa is a warm, welcoming day spa and boutique dedicated to beauty, relaxation and wellness.

Our spa features 3 pedicure chairs and 2 manicure stations. We take pride in providing exceptional client experiences, building lasting relationships and creating a friendly, supportive environment for both our clients and team members.

We are looking for a talented, passionate Nail Technician who takes pride in their work and enjoys making clients feel beautiful and cared for.

What you'll do

  • Provide high-quality manicures, pedicures and gel nail applications.
  • Nail extensions, nail art and seasonal designs are a welcome addition.
  • Maintain excellent sanitation and tool-disinfection standards between every client.
  • Keep your workstation clean, organized and professional.
  • Build positive relationships with clients and recommend appropriate at-home nail care, products and complementary spa services.
  • Work collaboratively with the team and contribute to maintaining a welcoming spa environment.

What we're looking for

  • Valid provincial nail technician certification or applicable professional credentials.
  • Minimum 3 years of professional experience.
  • Excellent attention to detail and time-management skills.
  • Friendly, caring and positive attitude.
  • Passion for beauty, wellness and customer service.
  • Ability to work independently as well as part of a team.

Existing clientele, front desk or retail experience is an asset.

What we offer

  • Competitive 50% commission + tips.
  • Flexible full-time or part-time schedule.
  • Supportive, friendly and team-oriented environment.
  • Opportunity for ongoing skill development and training.
  • 30% discount on all retail products.
  • Access to spa services and special family discounts.

Flexible work arrangement: we operate under a contractor agreement. An independent room/station rental arrangement may also be available.

Eyelash Technician

We are looking for a skilled, detail-oriented Eyelash Technician to join Vesna Beauty Spa in Cobble Hill.

Key responsibilities

  • Client consultation: assess client features, eye shapes and natural lash health to recommend appropriate lash lengths, curls and styles.
  • Lash application: apply individual synthetic or silk eyelash extensions with precision and safety.
  • Sanitation & safety: strictly sanitize and sterilize tools, tweezers, bed and workstation.
  • Aftercare education: educate clients on proper home maintenance and recommend products.

Qualifications & requirements

  • Certification: Eyelash Extension Certification.
  • Technical skills: proficiency in classic, hybrid and/or volume lash mapping and isolation.
  • Attention to detail: steady hands, good eyesight and patience for meticulous multi-hour applications.
  • Communication: friendly, professional demeanor with strong customer service skills.
  • Availability: flexibility to work some evenings or Saturdays/Sundays.

An existing client base and some front desk or retail experience are an asset.

What we offer

  • Competitive compensation: 50% commission plus tips, or possibly room rental
  • A supportive, team-oriented spa environment
  • Ongoing skill development and training
  • 30% discount on all products
  • Access to all spa services and a family discount
